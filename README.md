## 🧠 About Me

I'm a technology professional passionate about **Artificial Intelligence, research, and engineering intelligent systems**. I enjoy exploring complex problems, understanding them from first principles, and transforming ideas into practical solutions that create meaningful impact.

Currently, I work as an **AI Officer at Stavya Spine Hospital & Research Institute**, where I contribute to healthcare technology, AI-driven innovation, intelligent systems development, and digital transformation initiatives. My work brings together technology, problem-solving, and the opportunity to explore how AI can support real-world healthcare and research workflows.

Previously, I worked as a **Research Intern at the Space Applications Centre (SAC), ISRO, Ahmedabad**. During this experience, I contributed to **FMCW LiDAR preprocessing research and solution optimization**, strengthening my interest in applied research, engineering challenges, and scientific problem-solving.

I believe meaningful innovation comes from combining **strong fundamentals, curiosity, disciplined experimentation, and responsible engineering**. I am continuously learning, building, documenting, and improving—one problem and one project at a time.

- 🔭 **Currently working on:** AI-powered healthcare systems and intelligent applications
- 🧪 **Research interests:** Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, and medical imaging
- 🧩 **Exploring:** Multimodal AI, Generative AI, RAG, and intelligent agents
- 🛠️ **Enjoy building:** Practical tools, data-driven applications, and research prototypes
- 📚 **Learning philosophy:** Fundamentals first. Experiment consistently. Improve continuously.
- ✍️ **Also:** Published Author

> 💡 *Curiosity drives discovery. Research builds understanding. Engineering turns ideas into impact.*
