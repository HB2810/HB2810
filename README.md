# Het Bhatt

### AI Officer | Applied AI & Research | Intelligent Systems | Former Research Intern @ ISRO SAC

I work at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence, engineering, and applied research**, with a focus on turning technical ideas into practical systems.

Currently, I serve as an **AI Officer at Stavya Spine Hospital & Research Institute**, where I contribute to healthcare technology, AI-driven initiatives, intelligent systems, and digital transformation. I am particularly interested in applying AI to real operational and research problems while keeping solutions practical, responsible, and deployable.

Previously, I was a **Research Intern at the Space Applications Centre (SAC), ISRO, Ahmedabad**, where I contributed to research involving **FMCW LiDAR preprocessing** and solution optimization for complex technical challenges.

My interests span **Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, Data Science, Multimodal AI, Generative AI, and intelligent systems architecture**. I enjoy experimenting, building, evaluating, and learning continuously — especially where research can translate into useful real-world applications.
